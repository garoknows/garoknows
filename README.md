# Hi, I'm Garo

Second-generation operator at **[Haig's Quality Printing](https://haigsprinting.com)**, a family-owned commercial print shop in Las Vegas running for 45+ years. After ~15 years on the factory floor, I'm building software that brings AI to the shop floor and the front office.

## What I'm building

**[Printereum](https://printereum.com)**: AI-powered quoting and operations for commercial print shops. Learns a shop's real pricing from decades of quote history and turns incoming requests into ready-to-send quotes.

**[Neuramate](https://neuramate.com)**: Skills-as-a-Service. Captures expert operator knowledge with wearable vision so it can be turned into work instructions and, eventually, robot skills before it walks out the door. Member of NVIDIA Inception.

**[Stippled](https://stippled.com)**: Custom packaging, designed online. Pick a box, upload your art, see it in 3D, order, and it's printed and converted in-house.

**ScanDesk**: A local, private AI pipeline that turns scanned business mail and statements into reviewed, ready-to-import accounting entries.

**Shop Autopilot**: Automated marketing for a print shop. Job samples dropped in a folder get branded and posted on a schedule.

## How I work

Operator first, software second. Everything I build gets tested on a real production floor before it's called done.
